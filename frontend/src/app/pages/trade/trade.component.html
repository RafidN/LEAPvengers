Trading terms

Plain-language definitions for the terms used on this ticket.

Instrument

The asset you are trading, such as a stock, currency pair, or cryptocurrency.

Quote

The latest available price for one unit of an instrument.

Quantity

How many units you want to buy or sell.

Estimated cost

Quantity multiplied by the current quote. The final price can change.

Holdings

The assets and quantities currently owned in your account.

Execution

When the platform completes the order at an available price.

Proceeds

The estimated money received from selling an asset.

Market hours

The times an exchange is open for normal trading. Crypto and some FX markets differ.
